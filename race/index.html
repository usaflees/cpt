    Coin Race

    Pick two to six coins and a start line, then watch them race live, trade by trade, on prices from Coinbase, Binance and Kraken. Every race is a link you can send.

      How the race works

      • 24 h ago starts every coin at its price 24 hours ago (from the exchanges' rolling 24-hour change), so the race is already under way.
      • 1 h ago starts from Binance's price one hour ago. Coins without a Binance market show "—" for this start line.
      • Start now lines everyone up at their current price; the clock shows how long the race has run.

      Each bar grows right of the centre line when the coin is up since the start, left when it is down. The scale adjusts so the leader fits. The race shows what prices did; it doesn't predict anything.
