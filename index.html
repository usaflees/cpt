How CryptoPriceTicker.com works

⚡ Straight from the exchanges

Your browser opens WebSocket connections to Coinbase, Binance and Kraken and shows each trade as it happens. The small CB/BN/KR badge on every row shows which exchange sent the latest price.

📊 Market cap, volume & liquidity

Market cap is the live price × circulating supply (supply data last refreshed recently). Volume is the 24h total of the coin's USD/USDT markets on those three exchanges. “Est. liquidity” is a rough estimate (6.5% of that volume); each coin page shows real order-book depth within ±2% measured from Binance.

🔒 Private by design

No account and no ads. Your watchlist, bag quantities and alerts are saved only in this browser (localStorage). A sync link copies them to another device; anyone with the link can see them.

Frequently asked questions

Are these prices real-time?

Yes. Prices update tick-by-tick from the exchanges' public WebSocket feeds, typically within a second of a trade. If a feed disconnects, its badge at the top switches to RETRY and reconnects automatically.

Why is a coin's price slightly different on each exchange?

Every exchange has its own order book, so prices differ a little; Binance also quotes in USDT rather than US dollars. Open the 🔔 inspector or a coin's page to see each exchange's price and the cross-exchange spread.

How do price alerts work?

Set a USD target in the 🔔 inspector. When the live price crosses it, you hear a beep and see a notification. Alerts are stored in your browser and only fire while this page is open.

Can I add a live crypto ticker to my own website?

Yes, for free. Use Get Widget to choose a scrolling bar, a card grid or a single-coin card, then paste the HTML snippet into your page. The widget runs in a lazy-loaded iframe, so it adds no scripts to your site.

Is this investment advice?

No. Data comes from third-party exchanges and may be delayed, incomplete or wrong. Always verify prices before trading.
