How to add it

  1. Click Build your widget, pick a layout, coins, theme and currency.
  2. Click Copy HTML.
  3. Paste it where you want the ticker:
    • WordPress: add a Custom HTML block and paste the snippet.
    • Ghost, Webflow, Squarespace, Wix: use the HTML / Embed / Code block.
    • Notion: type /embed and paste just the iframe URL (the address that starts with https://cryptopriceticker.com/embed/).
    • Plain HTML or docs sites: paste it anywhere in the page body.

The widget lives in a lazy-loaded iframe, so it adds no JavaScript to your page and doesn't slow down your first paint. It works on phones and in dark or light designs (pick the matching theme).

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no fees, ads, sign-ups or usage limits. The snippet includes a small "Live prices by CryptoPriceTicker.com" credit link; you're welcome to keep it, restyle it or remove it.

Where do the prices come from?

Directly from the public market-data feeds of Coinbase, Binance and Kraken, streamed into the widget in your visitors' browsers. See how the data works.

Does it track my visitors?

No. The widget sets no cookies and stores nothing about your visitors. As with any embedded content, their browsers connect to our site and to the exchanges to load prices.

Which coins can I show?

Any of the 110+ coins on the home page, in US dollars or priced in BTC, ETH or SOL.
